Who I am

Born in a small town in northwestern Greece, I was exposed to operations from a very young age at my family's restaurant. Equipped with the problem solving skills of my chemistry degree, I went on to inform operational decisions with actionable data insights, first at one of Southeastern Europe's largest energy companies and then at Greece's largest university, working with cross functional teams from refinery operations to faculty and researchers on European funded projects. My passion for data driven decisions brought me to the University of Maryland, where I trained formally and added new tools to my analytical toolkit. I now apply them at the Foundation for Food and Agriculture Research in Washington, DC, working at the intersection of people, data analytics and AI.

Predicting seven obesity levels from body measurements and lifestyle habits

A team of six compared five supervised models and K-Means clustering on a benchmark dataset of 2,111 survey records from Mexico, Peru and Colombia. A Random Forest scored best at 93.6% accuracy, and clustering surfaced four lifestyle groups. BMI was left out on purpose to avoid label leakage.
